What does it install?

The NexGuard app, and the small agent it sets up: it runs in the background and sends how busy the CPU, memory and graphics are, the battery level, free storage, the system version, and whether it's on Wi-Fi or wired (with the Wi-Fi name where the system allows). It doesn't read your files, see your screen or record keystrokes. You can see it in Task Scheduler on Windows, in ~/Library/LaunchAgents on a Mac, or as a user service on Linux. The app shows its status and log.
