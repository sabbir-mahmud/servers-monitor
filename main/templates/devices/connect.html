What does it install?

A small check-in that runs as you every 5 minutes and sends how busy the CPU, memory and graphics are, the battery level, free storage, the system version, and whether it's on Wi-Fi or wired (with the Wi-Fi name where the system allows). It doesn't read your files, see your screen or record keystrokes. You can see it in Task Scheduler on Windows, in ~/Library/LaunchAgents on a Mac, or as a user timer on Linux.
